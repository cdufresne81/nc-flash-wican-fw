# v1.22.1 - A flash no longer depends on your laptop staying awake

One fix, aimed at the moment when interrupting a flash costs you an engine computer.

**If you use an older NC Flash, this release is for you.** You do not need to update the PC tool.

## The problem

Before it can take a new ROM, the ECU has to wipe its own memory. It goes completely silent while it does that - about **13 seconds** on a full ROM.

The adapter is happy to wait. Your PC tool is the one counting. **NC Flash 2.12.0 and older give up after 30 seconds of silence** and walk away. The ECU has already erased itself by then, so it is left with nothing to run, and only a full re-flash brings it back.

13 seconds against a 30-second limit is not a comfortable margin. A slower erase or a busier bus and it is gone.

## The fix

The adapter now **sends a short progress line every 5 seconds while the ECU is quiet**. Your tool always has something to read, so its timer never runs out. This works with every version of NC Flash, old or new - nothing changes on the PC side.

## It also fixes something that could bite anyone

There was a second way to lose an ECU, and it had nothing to do with old tools.

Once the erase has started, the ECU is committed. If your Wi-Fi hiccuped for two seconds during the one to four minutes it takes to write the ROM - or your laptop got busy, or the lid closed - the adapter **gave up in the middle of the write**.

That is fixed. **Once the ECU has started erasing, the adapter finishes the job no matter what your computer does.** It keeps sending progress, but if nobody is listening it just carries on and writes the ROM anyway. Afterwards the log tells you what happened:

```
PC tool stopped listening at block 704/1022 -- the flash finished without it
```

The v1.22.0 notes called this gap deliberately open. It is now closed.

## How it was tested

Both cases were run on a real engine computer with a real, unmodified NC Flash 2.12.0:

- **Full ROM with the old tool** - 12.9-second erase, two progress lines sent through the silence, flash completed normally.
- **Full ROM with the tool killed at 69%** - the adapter wrote the remaining 318 blocks on its own and the ECU was fine. On v1.22.0 that same test leaves the ECU unbootable.

Losing the connection mid-write cost 0.3 seconds.

## Install

**Over Wi-Fi (recommended):** open the device page, go to **System > Update Firmware**, upload `wican-fw_obd_pro_v1_22_1.bin`.

**Over USB (serial):** `bootloader.bin` at `0x0`, `partition-table.bin` at `0x8000`, `ota_data_initial.bin` at `0xd000`, `wican-fw_obd_pro_v1_22_1.bin` at `0x10000`.

## Honest notes

**Known issue: the datalogger can stay paused after an interrupted flash.** If NC Flash disappears mid-session, the adapter is supposed to notice and start logging again by itself after about 80 seconds. It does not always do that while the ECU is still talking on the bus - it can stay paused, looking perfectly healthy, and record nothing. Restarting NC Flash clears it, and so does rebooting the adapter. Being tracked as issue #131.

**Fault-code reads and ROM reads still leave no trace in the log.** You will see that a host claimed the bus, not what it did with it.
